Donor Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Thank you for giving through HopeBridge. This policy explains how we handle your gift and what you can expect from us.

Our commitment

HopeBridge is a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting individuals and families facing financial and medical challenges. We aim to be transparent, to treat every patient with dignity, and to make sure gifts contribute to real, documented needs.

How your gift is used

  • Each campaign is backed by documentation of the medical need.
  • HopeBridge pays the hospital, clinic or pharmacy directly for the documented care. Money is not handed to individuals.
  • Gifts made toward a named patient are used for that patient's care.

If a campaign is fully funded or cannot go ahead

If a patient's needs are fully met, or a campaign can no longer proceed, remaining funds are used for another verified patient with similar needs.

Receipts and tax information

Eligible donations made to HopeBridge may be tax-deductible to the extent permitted by applicable U.S. tax laws. Donors should retain their donation receipts for their records and consult a qualified tax professional regarding their individual tax situation. We email a receipt for every gift, so please use the confirmation form on our donate page so we can reach you.

Payment fees

Payment providers such as Sentz may charge processing fees, which can reduce the amount we receive.

Gifts made by mistake

If you gave by mistake or entered the wrong amount, contact us as soon as possible and we will review your request.

Donor privacy and recognition

We do not publish donor names without permission. If you prefer to give anonymously, tell us and we will respect that. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information.

Patient dignity

We share patient stories and photos only with consent, and we present them respectfully. We do not use images or details that could embarrass or endanger a patient or family.
