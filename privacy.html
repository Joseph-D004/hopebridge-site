Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

HopeBridge is a nonprofit organization that connects donors with verified medical cases. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • From donors: your name, email address, gift amount and payment reference when you fill in our confirmation form.
  • From subscribers: your email address if you sign up for updates.
  • From patients and families: health and personal details needed to verify a case, shared only with consent.
  • From visitors: basic technical information, such as browser type, which our hosting provider may record.

How we use it

  • To thank you, send receipts and confirm your gift.
  • To send updates and stories, if you subscribed.
  • To verify patient needs and pay hospitals, clinics or pharmacies directly.
  • To keep records, meet legal duties and prevent fraud.

Patient privacy and consent

We share a patient's name, photo or story on this site only with their consent, or a parent or guardian's consent for a child. We share only what is needed to explain the need, and we respect a patient's request to remove their story.

Payments

Gifts are processed by third-party providers such as Sentz, and later Flutterwave and PayPal, on their own pages under their own privacy policies. HopeBridge does not see or store your card or account details.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • hospitals, clinics and pharmacies, where needed to pay for a patient's care;
  • service providers that help us run the site, send email or process payments;
  • authorities, where the law requires it.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or system can be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from updates at any time by emailing us.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and we will respond as the law requires. We may need to keep some records, such as donation receipts.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Information about a child patient is collected only from a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
